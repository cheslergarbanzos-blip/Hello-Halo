The Creamiest Halo-Halo Experience

Iloilo's Favorite Halo-Halo & Desserts.

Hello Halo is a beloved dessert shop in Iloilo City serving a colorful variety of halo-halo and other cool Filipino treats, made fresh daily with real ube, sweet beans, and creamy leche flan.

Visit Us Today

Why Hello Halo?

We hand-mix every halo-halo with real sweetened fruits, beans, and jellies, then top it off with creamy ube ice cream. Our mission is simple: bring a little sweetness and a lot of color to every Ilonggo's day.
