Our Story

“The Creamiest Halo-Halo in Iloilo City” — a positioning Hello Halo repeatedly uses on its own social media.

Hello Halo began in 2018 as a small dessert stall along a busy street in Iloilo City, where the founder sold homemade halo-halo out of a single ice box during the hot summer months. The refreshing mix of shaved ice, sweet beans, and creamy ube quickly became a favorite among students and jeepney drivers passing by.

As demand grew, the stall moved into a proper storefront, allowing the shop to expand its menu beyond the classic halo-halo. New Filipino favorites such as taho, buko pandan, and mais con yelo were added, all made using traditional recipes passed down from the founder's family.

True to its Ilonggo roots, Hello Halo sources its ube, saba bananas, and sweet beans from local Iloilo suppliers, supporting nearby farmers while keeping every serving fresh and authentic.

Growing Beyond Iloilo

Today, Hello Halo has grown into a well-loved dessert destination in the city, known for its generous toppings, colorful presentation, and friendly service. Families, students, and tourists alike make it a point to stop by for a cold treat any time of the year.

Hello Halo is operated under Nothing But Desserts Shoppe, Inc., the registered trademark owner listed with the Philippine Intellectual Property Office, and now operates multiple branches across Iloilo City and nearby towns such as Molo, Jaro, Villa/Arevalo, Pavia, and Oton. Visit our Contact page for the full list of branches.

Hello Halo has since grown beyond Iloilo, with newer branches now open in Bacolod, Cebu, and Passi City — a sign that its take on halo-halo has found an audience well beyond its original city.

Looking forward, Hello Halo hopes to continue growing while staying true to the same handmade, homegrown quality that started it all.
